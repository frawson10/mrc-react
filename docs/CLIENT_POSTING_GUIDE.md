# Posting announcements and reflections

Monastery of the Risen Christ | Eidos editing guide

## Start here

Each announcement or reflection now has its own page. Visitors see a short introduction in a listing and click through to read the full post. Write the post once; the listings and homepage highlights link to that page.

| What you are posting | Website section | Collection value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An announcement, event invitation, or community update | News & Events | news |
| A reflection, homily, reading recommendation, or resource | Reflections & Resources | reflections |

**The usual workflow:** Create a draft, create the post's page, add its heading and writing, place it in the section's list, preview, then publish.

**Saving and publishing are separate.** Save Changes stores your draft. It does not update the public website.

## 1. Create your draft and page

1. Sign in to Eidos and select the Monastery of the Risen Christ organization. Open **Editor**.
2. Choose **Create New Branch**. A branch is your draft workspace. Give it a short **Branch Title**, such as **Post Advent Invitation**, then choose **Create Branch**. Branch titles use letters and spaces only, up to 30 characters.
3. Choose **Add Page**. Enter a **Page Title** and a short **Page Description**. Use the post's own title, such as **Advent Prayer Evening 2026**, rather than a general title such as Announcements.
4. Check **Page Preview**, which shows the page address Eidos will create. Use a distinct title for each post; include the year for recurring events. Do not create a second page with an address already in use. Choose **Create Page**.

Keep a note of the address in Page Preview. You will select that same address when adding this post to a list. New addresses may be short names such as **advent-prayer-evening-2026**, even though older posts have addresses beginning with news/ or reflections/. Both work.

Your new page needs two sections, called blocks: **Article Header** for its title and short introduction, followed by **Text Section** for the full writing. Continue on the next page.

## 2. Add the heading and full writing

On the new page, choose **Add Block To Page**. Select **Article Header** under **Select Block Type**, give it a **Block Nickname** such as Article details, and choose **Add Block**. Nicknames help you identify sections inside Eidos; they are not the public headline.

Expand the block using its triangle icon, then complete these fields:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The public headline. Use the same title you entered when creating the page. |
| Collection | Enter exactly **news** for announcements or **reflections** for reflections. Do not add spaces. This determines which section includes the post. |
| Category | The type of post. For news, use **Event** or **Community** as appropriate. For reflections, use an existing category such as **Reflection**, **Homily**, **Reading list**, or **Resource**. Match existing spelling and capitalization to keep the filters tidy. |
| Author | The writer's name, or Monastery of the Risen Christ for a community announcement. |
| Date Label | The date visitors should see. For an event, include its date and year; put the time and location in the full writing. This field does not schedule publication or sort the list. |
| Summary | One or two short sentences introducing the post. This appears in the listing and at the top of the article. You can reuse the Page Description here. |
| Image | Optional. Upload a photograph or choose **Choose From Library** to reuse one. The article and some highlighted layouts use this image. |
| Image Alt | If you use an image, describe it briefly, for example People gathering in the monastery chapel. |

For a new original post, leave **Original Title**, **Original ID**, **Video**, **Resource**, and **Source Credit** empty unless they are needed. Preserve existing credits when editing older material.

### Add the full writing

1. Choose **Add Block To Page** again, select **Text Section**, give it a nickname such as Main text, and choose **Add Block**.
2. Select **Default** in its **Style** menu. Put the full post in **Body**. For an announcement, include what is happening, when, where, who is welcome, and any registration or contact details.
3. Leave the other fields empty unless you need them. The Article Header already supplies the main title and summary. Additional Text Sections can have their own **Title** for subheadings.
4. Keep Article Header first and the writing below it. Use the drag handles to reorder blocks if needed. An **Image Gallery** block can follow the writing when you have several photographs.

For a video or resource link, expand **Video** or **Resource** in Article Header, select **External Link** under Style, enter **Button Text** and the full **External URL** beginning with https://. Leave these fields blank when no extra link is needed.

## 3. Put the post near the top of its section

A post with the correct Collection appears automatically in its section's list. **New posts are added after the existing ordered entries. Dates do not move them to the top.** To give a new post a prominent position:

1. In the same draft, select **News & Events** or **Reflections & Resources** from the page tabs. Use the double-arrow page selector if the page is not visible.
2. Find and expand **Article Index**, then expand **Items**.
3. Choose **Add Item Here** above the first entry. If you use **Add To List** instead, drag the new item to the top afterward.
4. Expand the new item, then its **Page** field. In **Target Page**, select the address noted in Page Preview when you created the post. Choose the individual article, rather than the News or Reflections listing page.
5. Add the post only once. Leave the Article Index's Collection value unchanged.

The list reads the headline and summary from your article. You do not need to paste the writing into this list.

## Editing an existing post

Create a new draft, or reopen your unfinished draft using **Select Branch To Edit**. Select the individual article from the page tabs or double-arrow selector. Update **Article Header** for the headline, summary, author, date, or main image; update **Text Section** for the full writing. Then save, preview, and publish as described on the next page.

The listing cards update from the article. You do not need a new page for a correction. Changing the headline does not change the page address; ask Symposio for help if you also need its address or search title changed.

## Optional: highlight a post

### On the homepage

1. Open **Home** in the same draft. Find the **Featured Articles** block nicknamed **A living community.** for announcements, or **Read. Listen. Reflect.** for reflections.
2. Expand **Items**. To replace an older highlight, change that item's **Page > Target Page** to your article. To add another, use **Add To List** and select your article. Drag items to change their order.
3. **Label** is optional button wording, such as Read the reflection. Leave the section's heading and Style unchanged. Preview the result to check the layout.

Publishing an article does not automatically feature it on Home. Removing a homepage highlight leaves the article available in its section.

### In the large feature on News & Events

Open that page's **Featured Articles** block with **Style: Feature**, initially nicknamed **A renewed space for prayer.** Change its **Items > Page > Target Page** to the article you want to highlight. Also update or clear the block's **Title**, **Eyebrow**, and **Body**. When empty, these use the article's headline, category, and summary; filled fields override them. The photograph comes from the article.

## 4. Preview and publish

1. Choose **Save Changes** and wait for saving to finish.
2. Choose **Live Testing** to open your saved draft. Refresh an already-open preview.
3. Check the listing, full article, links, and any homepage highlight on desktop and a phone.
4. Return to Eidos. Choose **Create Merge Request** and follow its prompts to queue the saved draft.
5. Choose **Back To Requests**, find your request, and select **Publish Changes Immediately**. If your team has a designated publisher, leave it for that person. To publish later, choose **Schedule Release** and verify the date and time.
6. When the release status reads **LIVE**, use **View Live Site** to check the public post. Live Testing is a draft preview.

### Before you publish

- The Collection is exactly news or reflections.
- The title, summary, author, and date are correct.
- An event includes its year, time, location, and contact or booking details.
- The full writing is in Text Section, below Article Header.
- The post is in the intended position in its section's list.
- Images, links, and any homepage highlight have been checked on a phone.

## Common questions

| What you notice | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The post is missing from its section. | Confirm it has an Article Header with the exact Collection value. Save and refresh Live Testing. Clear any category or search filter on the website. |
| The post appears at the bottom. | Add it once to Article Index > Items and move it to the desired position. Changing Date Label will not reorder it. |
| The large News feature still has the old headline. | Its own Title may override the article headline. Update or clear that field; check Eyebrow and Body too. |
| The public site is unchanged. | Confirm the draft was saved, a merge request was created, and the release was published and reached LIVE. |
| A page is locked. | Another draft may own it, or yours may be out of date. Check with your team or Symposio. Do not create a second copy of the post. |

**After an event:** Update outdated wording, remove its homepage highlight, and move its entry lower in Article Index. Keep the article as a record of community life. Removing it from Items alone does not hide it: the section also finds matching articles automatically. Ask Symposio about removing a post completely.
